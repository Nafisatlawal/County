# Monroe County Car Crash Analysis Dashboard
This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of car crash incidents recorded in Monroe County, offering insights into crash frequency by time, location, collision type, and yearly trends. The goal is to help local authorities, city planners, and public safety officials understand patterns and prioritize safety interventions.

## Key Statistics
- Total Car Crashes Recorded: 52,627

-Top Collision Type:
1. 2-Car Crashes: ~37,000 incidents
2. 1-Car Crashes: ~10,000 incidents

## Crash Distribution by Week:
- Weekdays account for 75.49% of all car crashes, indicating a significantly higher risk during workdays.

## Top Crash Location:
- E 3rd Street reported the highest number of crashes, identifying it as a high-risk roadway in the county.

## Year with Most Crashes:
- 2008 recorded the highest annual total of car crashes, suggesting a notable spike that year requiring further analysis.

## Time-Based Filtering
The dashboard features a combined slicer for Month and Day, enabling users to drill down into specific days of the week or time periods to see when crashes are most frequent.

## Visualizations Used
- Clustered Bar Charts: Crash count by collision type
- Area chart: Yearly trends in crash volumes
-Table showed reported location and number of car crash
- Pie Chart: Weekly report of car crash

## Slicer:
- Month + Day Combination for granular filtering

## Built With
- Power BI Desktop – Data visualization and dashboard development
- Power Query – Data transformation and cleaning
- DAX – For calculated fields and KPIs
